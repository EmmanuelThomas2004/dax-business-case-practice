# DAX Business Case Practice

A collection of **5 practical DAX business questions** designed to strengthen Power BI and DAX problem-solving skills.

## 📌 About This Repository

This repository focuses on solving common business analytics problems using **DAX in Power BI**.

The exercises cover:

* Aggregation
* `CALCULATE()`
* Customer analysis
* Ranking
* `FILTER()`
* Variables
* Iterators such as `SUMX()` and `AVERAGEX()`

## 📊 Business Questions

### 1. Total Sales

Calculate the total sales from the `Sales` column.

**Concepts:** `SUM()`

### 2. Sales in 2025

Calculate the total sales generated during 2025.

**Concepts:** `CALCULATE()`, date filtering

### 3. Repeat Customers

Find the number of customers who placed more than one order.

**Concepts:** `FILTER()`, `VALUES()`, `CALCULATE()`, `COUNTROWS()`

### 4. Top 5 Products by Sales

Rank products based on their total sales and identify the top 5 products.

**Concepts:** `RANKX()`, `ALL()`

### 5. Sales Above Average

Calculate the total sales generated by products whose sales are above the average product sales.

**Concepts:** `VAR`, `AVERAGEX()`, `FILTER()`, `SUMX()`
